# Before / After — Reasoning Examples

## Example 1 — SaaS Landing Hero

### Before (classic AI default)

- Dark background with purple→blue gradient orb
- Gradient text headline: “Unlock the future of seamless invoicing”
- Small pill badge: “AI-POWERED”
- Two buttons of similar weight
- Three glass cards below with 🔒 📈 ⚡ and generic claims
- Inter everywhere

**Why it fails:**  
Multiple Tier-1 signals. No real thesis. Signature missing. Copy is pure statistical SaaS language. Hierarchy is flat.

### After (strict direction)

**Visual Thesis:**  
Quiet, paper-like authority. One sharp navy accent. Typography does the work. No decoration.

**Tokens:**  
- Accent: `#0A2540`  
- Neutrals: warm off-white + deep charcoal  
- Type: Newsreader (display) + Inter (body, used only for UI)  
- Spacing: 4/8 scale  
- Radius: 4px  
- Shadow: none

**Signature:**  
Large, specific product screenshot of a real invoice with one annotation callout.

**Result:**  
Headline states the actual job (“Get paid without chasing clients”). One primary button. Supporting proof is real numbers or a real quote. No cards, no emoji, no gradient. The screenshot is the memorable element.

## Example 2 — Dashboard

### Before

- Purple accent on sidebar active state
- Soft glow under metric cards
- Identical rounded cards with thin borders
- Low-contrast grey labels
- Emoji status indicators

### After

**Visual Thesis:**  
Dense, calm data surface. High contrast. Color only for status and primary actions.

**Tokens:**  
- Accent: single brand green for positive deltas and primary buttons  
- Neutrals: true near-black and precise greys meeting AA  
- Type: strong mono for numbers + clean sans for labels  
- Radius: 2px  
- Shadow: none (borders + background shift only)

**Signature:**  
One custom chart treatment (e.g., specific line weight + direct labels instead of legend).

**Result:**  
Metrics are readable at a glance. Status uses semantic color only. No glow, no glass, no decorative radius. Empty states explain what to do next.

---

## Key pattern

Every successful after follows the same sequence:

1. Name the thesis
2. Lock tokens
3. Choose one signature
4. Remove every element that does not serve hierarchy or the signature
5. Write real content

The before versions skip steps 1–3 and therefore collapse into the statistical average.
